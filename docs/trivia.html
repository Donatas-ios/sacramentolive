🎲 Sacramento Trivia

Daily selection of fun facts about California's capital · Updated July 10, 2026

🎪

State Fair

The California State Fair has been held in Sacramento every year since 1854
Cal Expo hosts the California State Fair each summer, drawing nearly 500,000 visitors. It's one of the largest state fairs in the US, featuring agriculture, entertainment, and food.
🎸

Music

Sacramento has produced major music acts including Tesla, Deftones, and Cake
The Sacramento music scene punches above its weight. Deftones formed at Del Campo High School in the 1980s and went on to global fame with alternative metal.
☀️

Sunny City

Sacramento averages 265 sunny days per year — sunnier than Miami
Summers are hot and dry with temperatures regularly exceeding 100°F. The Mediterranean climate makes Sacramento one of the sunniest large cities in the United States.
📚

Education

Sacramento State was founded in 1947 and is the only university in the California State system named after a city
California State University, Sacramento (Sac State) serves over 30,000 students and is a major economic driver for the region, contributing over $2 billion annually to the local economy.
🏘️

Victorian Architecture

Sacramento's 'Poverty Ridge' has one of California's finest Victorian home collections
The area around 16th and H Streets (nicknamed ironically for its wealthy residents) contains hundreds of beautifully preserved Victorian mansions from the 1800s.
🚂

Transcontinental Railroad

Sacramento was the western terminus of the First Transcontinental Railroad
In 1863, Sacramento was chosen as the starting point for the Central Pacific Railroad. The railroad was completed in 1869, connecting the country coast to coast for the first time.
🏀

Sacramento Kings

The Sacramento Kings are the NBA's oldest franchise in continuous operation
Founded in 1923 as the Rochester Seagrams, the Kings moved to Sacramento in 1985. They play at the Golden 1 Center, one of the most technologically advanced arenas in the world.
🏗️

Old Sacramento

Old Sacramento is a 28-acre National Historic Landmark
Old Sacramento preserves the look of the 1850s Gold Rush era with wooden sidewalks, brick buildings, and cobblestone streets along the Sacramento River waterfront.

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

1. What year did Sacramento Republic FC set a USL attendance world record?

In 2014, Sacramento Republic FC drew over 11,000 fans to their USL home opener — a world record for the league at the time.

2. What percentage of America's processed tomatoes come from the Sacramento Valley?

The Sacramento Valley produces about 90% of all processed tomatoes in the United States.

3. In what year was the Pony Express replaced by the telegraph?

The Pony Express ran from April 1860 to October 1861, when the transcontinental telegraph made it obsolete after just 18 months.

4. What nickname is Sacramento known by because of its trees?

Sacramento's urban forest of over 1 million trees earns it the nickname 'City of Trees' — with more canopy per capita than most cities worldwide.

5. Which two rivers meet near downtown Sacramento?

The Sacramento River and American River converge just west of downtown, making Sacramento a true River City.
